Common size

Shrink a photo to a size commonly used for sharing and recompress it, so the original resolution and the camera's compression settings no longer point to the phone model.

How to use it

  1. Choose a photo taken on a phone. JPEG, HEIC, AVIF and WebP all work.
  2. Pick a size for the long edge and press “Shrink and recompress”.
  3. Download the smaller photo. It is always saved as JPEG, without the original metadata.

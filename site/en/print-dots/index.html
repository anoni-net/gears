Printer tracking dots

Remove the faint yellow dots colour laser printers put on paper before you hand over a scan or photo.

How to use it

  1. Choose a scan or photo of a printed page, as an image or PDF.
  2. Switch to “Yellow only” and zoom in to look for a regular pattern of tiny dots.
  3. Choose “Remove faint yellow” or “Black and white”, press “Apply” and download.

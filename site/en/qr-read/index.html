QR code and barcode reader

Read QR codes and barcodes with your camera or a photo, and see what they hold before opening a link, paying or sharing.

How to use it

  1. Press “Scan with camera”, or “Choose a photo” to read a screenshot or photo.
  2. What was read is split into fields, and things like payment codes and friend invites are flagged.
  3. Check it before copying. A link can go to the link checker first.

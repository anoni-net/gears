Remove metadata

Remove the location and phone model from photos, and the author and editing history from documents and media.

How to use it

  1. Choose photos, documents, audio or video. You can pick several at once.
  2. The screen lists what the file holds, such as the location, phone model and author. Press “Remove” to clear it all.
  3. Download or share the cleaned file. The original stays in the tray, and you can undo the step from the history.

Before you hand it over

Remove details that could identify you, or hand over only the part that is needed.

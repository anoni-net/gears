Nearby transfer

Connect two devices to the same Wi-Fi or phone hotspot, pair them by scanning each other's QR code, then send files from the tray directly, without going through any server.

How to use it

  1. Put both devices on the same Wi-Fi or phone hotspot. On the sending device, first press “Choose files” to put the files in the tray.
  2. The sender presses “Send files from the tray”, the receiver presses “Receive files”, and each scans the other’s QR code to pair.
  3. Check that both confirmation codes match, then the receiver presses “Accept” and the files go into the tray.

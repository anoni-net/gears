anoni.net Gears

Strip metadata, redact personal details, encrypt files, transcribe audio with open-source on-device models, and check files, emails and links you receive.

Works in your browser

Every tool works as soon as you open it, with no install or sign-up. Files are processed in your browser, and Gears keeps working offline after the first visit.

Stronger with open-source on-device models

Tools marked with the brain icon can also use this device's computing power to run open-source models, for example to find faces in a photo or turn a recording into a transcript. A model is downloaded the first time it's needed and then stays on this device.

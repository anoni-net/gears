Link checker

Check against the 165 anti-fraud list, see the real domain, and remove tracking parameters.

How to use it

  1. Paste the link you want to check, or press “Read links from a screenshot” and choose a screenshot.
  2. The link is compared against Taiwan’s 165 anti-fraud list, and the real domain and any tracking codes are marked.
  3. Press “Copy the clean link” to get the link without tracking codes.

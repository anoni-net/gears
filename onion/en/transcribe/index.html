Transcript

Turn recordings and videos into timestamped text in Mandarin, Taiwanese Hokkien, Cantonese and other Asian languages.

How to use it

  1. Choose a recording or video.
  2. Pick the spoken language and a model, then press “Start transcribing”. The model is downloaded the first time.
  3. Check the transcript against the recording, then download or copy it as TXT or SRT subtitles.

Before you hand it over

Remove details that could identify you, or hand over only the part that is needed.

Remote transfer

When the other person isn't with you, create a link for them and send the files in your tray through the community's relay server, and exchange text messages or make a video call once connected.

How to use it

  1. Press “Choose files” to put the files in the tray, then press “Send files from the tray” to create a link. For messages or video only, press “Messages or video call only”.
  2. Send the link to the other person another way. They open it and press “Join”.
  3. Check that both confirmation codes match, then the other person presses “Accept” and the files go into their tray.

Send it safely

Encrypt before sending, so only the recipient can open it.
